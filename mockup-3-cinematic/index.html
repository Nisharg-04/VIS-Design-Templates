The Blueprint
विद्या विनय सम्पन्ने

Knowledge, adorned with humility.

Vinay International School stands as a beacon of holistic education, not only in Morbi city but across Gujarat, blending the best of traditional values and modern practices.

At VIS, learning is not just about academics. It is a transformative journey where students discover their purpose and create a meaningful impact in the world.

Our Purpose to shape them

To shape individuals who excel academically, think creatively and lead ethically, in a nurturing environment where every student discovers their unique potential and grows into a confident, compassionate and capable global citizen.

Our Vision to equip them

To be recognised as a premier institution that equips students with the knowledge, skills and values to succeed in a dynamic, interconnected world, fostering lifelong learners who contribute to their communities and beyond.

Our Mission to educate them

  • A balanced education blending academic excellence with creative exploration.
  • Holistic development through co-curricular activities and value-based education.
  • A culture of learning that embraces innovation, diversity and respect for all.
  • Skills and mindset for continuous learning and responsible leadership.
