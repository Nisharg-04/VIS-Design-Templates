Four ways to tell the Vinay story.

Each concept is a fully working homepage built from the 2026-27 brochure, with a loading animation, live notice board, admission enquiry, careers and contact. Open them on a laptop and a phone, then choose the direction that feels most like VIS. We can also combine elements from different concepts.

CONCEPT 01

Heritage Editorial“Vidya”

Dignified and premium. Ivory paper, navy ink and saffron-gold, with temple-arch image frames and chapters numbered like the brochure. It says “prestigious institution rooted in Indian values.”

Fraunces · Manrope
  • Loader: the Sanskrit motto writes itself, pillars rise, temple doors open
  • Horizontal-scroll journey from Nursery to Std. 12
  • Arch-framed leadership portraits

CONCEPT 02

Happy Vinayans“Spectrum”

Joyful and full of energy. Every section carries its brochure chapter colour, “3000+” is filled with real student photos, and sticker badges float around the hero. Built for parents and children to love at first sight.

Bricolage Grotesque · DM Sans
  • Loader: the smile draws in and a counter runs to 3000+
  • Header colour changes with every chapter
  • A sticker wall of 40+ SKILLkari programmes

CONCEPT 03

Campus Universe“Cinematic”

Bold and immersive. A dark, film-like experience with full-screen campus imagery, glowing statistics and an interactive 10-acre campus map. It positions VIS as Gujarat's most ambitious school.

Unbounded · Inter Tight
  • Loader: the campus assembles from a blueprint, then the camera flies in
  • Clickable campus map with every block explained
  • A medal vault with 833 medals counting up

CONCEPT 04

Clarity“Bento”

Crisp, modern and trustworthy. The school's own blue with a yellow highlighter, organised into neat tiles so parents find everything in seconds. The cleanest and most practical option.

Plus Jakarta Sans
  • Loader: the logo fills like water and becomes the header
  • 3-step admission wizard and searchable notice board
  • Facilities explorer with tabs for sports, arts and labs
