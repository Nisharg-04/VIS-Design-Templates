## Academic journey (4 stages)
1. **Pre-School (Nursery to Sr. KG): "Foundation for Life"**. "मस्ती की पाठशाला". "The Biggest & Most Trusted Pre School of Morbi". Play-way & concept-based learning, early literacy, numeracy & phonics, daily prayers & values, motor skills/art/stories. Skills: Physical, Language, Personality, Performing, Creative, Cognitive, Social & Life. Thoughtfully designed curriculum, special fitness & sports coach, activity camps for kids & mothers, well-trained caring teachers, safe and secure, burdenless activity-based learning. Campuses: City (D'N'D) near Sky Mall; Branch (D'N'D) Anupam Society; Branch (Kinderoo) Swachhta Marg.
2. **Primary (Std. 1 to 4): "Strong Basics. Strong Habits."**. "Beyond Books, We Believe in True Happiness". Reading, writing, mental maths; concept clarity; grammar booster; weekly assessments & remediation; arts, clubs & sports; joyful activity-based learning. Pillars: Happiness, Language Mastery, Training, Discipline, Values & Habits, Guidance.
3. **Middle School (Std. 5 to 8): "Skill-Building Years"**. "Beyond knowledge, we focus on skill building". Weekly science experiments & math mastery, application-based SST, weekly/unit/periodic tests & trackers, Discover series & early career exposure. Pillars: Skill, Time Management, Responsibility, Critical Thinking, Decision Making, Problem Solving.
4. **Secondary & Sr. Secondary (Std. 9 to 12): "Performance, Decision & Career Years"**. 1000-Day Journey (9 to 12), multi-round prelims & 2-hour challenge papers, daily revision sheets & doubt-solving hubs, 90-day board exam strategy, Science JEE/NEET foundation, Commerce Vi-CCC (GST, Stock Market, Entrepreneurship), Career Crafting. "No Other School Can Plan & Perform Like We Do".
   Flagship programs: **Taiyari Jeet Ki (Now or Never)**: high-intensity final-stretch preparation · **The Exam Express**: fast-track revision with continuous assessments · **The Summit of Solutions**: problem-solving initiative · **Career Crafting**: counselling, exposure, real-world insight · **Vi-CCC (Vinay International's Commerce Cultivation Course)**: premium commerce-readiness.
